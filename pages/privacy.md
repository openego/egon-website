## Privacy Policy

### 1. General

The protection of your personal data is important to us. We process your data exclusively in accordance with applicable data protection legislation, in particular the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

### 2. Contact Form

If you send us enquiries via our contact form, your data will be processed, stored and forwarded to us by the service Formpost.eu for the purpose of handling your request (Art. 6 (1) GDPR). Transmission to Formpost.eu takes place via the HTTPS protocol.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR (legitimate interest). The data is stored at Formpost.eu and by us until you request deletion.

Further information: [https://formpost.eu/datenschutz](https://formpost.eu/datenschutz)

### 3. Your Rights

You have the right to obtain information about your stored personal data, to request its correction or deletion, to restrict processing, and to data portability. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise your rights, please contact: info(ät)rl-institut.de
